# The two rooms: response tables

This is the design for the redesign. The unit is a *response*: a thing on the screen,
what it answers to, and what it does. If it is not in these tables it is not in the
build. The geometry of both rooms is frozen; the route and escape sweeps guard it.

Premise (replaces the salt-works in PREMISE.md when build 1 lands): treasure hunters
opened a vault; it sealed behind them; under it is an ancient city of the strange people
who built it, and of the hunters who came before and never left. No words, anywhere.
Inhabitants speak the way the plant does: a voice of syllables, a body that gestures, and
an answer to what you set down.

**The light rule.** Amber is flame, and flame is the hunters': your lamp, the campfire,
the seams in raw rock. Green-white is the city's own light: its glass lamps, the face,
the native's flank, the pigment of its murals, the fish. You learn who made a thing by
what colour it gives off.

**Zones.** Each tile belongs to VAULT (raw rock, timber shelves, amber) or CITY (dressed
masonry, stone cornices, green light, ashlar back wall with windows). Authored as a
handful of rectangles per room.

### The hunter (build 3)

Your size and your shape, with a pack and a hat's silhouette so you know it is not you.
Voice A: reedy, mid, two or three syllables, the plant's method. Never leaves the camp
(4 tiles), never touches you, never follows.

| state | what they do |
|---|---|
| idle | sits by the cold fire; every 20-40 s tends the cairn: lifts the top stone, turns it, sets it back; hums a syllable now and then |
| you within 5 tiles | looks up, one syllable, watches; then back to the cairn |
| a stone set within 2 tiles | walks to it, picks it up, places it on the cairn; a short phrase |
| you take a stone from the cairn | stands, a sharp syllable, head follows the stone; if you set it down within 4 tiles they take it back; further, they sit, and the cairn is one short |
| the fire catches | turns to it, sits closer, holds both hands to it, a long low phrase; hums more from then on |
| you stand on the bedroll | a syllable, and waits |

### The native (build 4)

26x9, pale, a frill at the head, three green lights along the flank. Voice B: a hollow low
tone with a whistle over it, two or three syllables; a long exhale when it breathes. Larger
than you, at home where you cannot stay. Never touches you.

| state | what it does |
|---|---|
| circuit | under the surface between cols 9 and 30 at rows 8-12, slow, turning at the ends |
| every 30-60 s | rises mid-pool and breathes: the exhale, a ring on the surface, bubbles |
| you floating | on its next pass it goes under you, close: a bulge on the surface, a muffled sound. It does not change course |
| you splash in | dives to the floor with a tail slap; stays down 15 s |
| your lamp at a water's edge, or floating | comes, holds under the light, flank pulsing; surfaces to look, eyes above the line; sings once, softly; stays while the light does |
| a stone sinks | goes to where it landed, noses it, leaves |
| you heavy on the floor | follows at 5 tiles, level with you, watching; never closer |
| the fish | scatter from it |

## What the engine needs

1. **A props layer.** A second text grid per room, one char per prop, drawn behind or in
   front of the tiles; a flat array with a small state each (sway, tip, lit, fallen).
2. **Text sprites.** The door, the face, the mural, the hunter and the native are rows of
   palette letters in the source, like the maps. Nothing streams.
3. **Zones.** Rectangles per room; stone, shelves and the back wall draw by zone.
4. **Two light bakes.** Warm and cool emitters accumulate separately and combine at the
   texture; dynamic points carry a warmth too (lamp, aura, fire warm; the native and the
   fixtures cool). The grate gets underlight from the cistern.
5. **Translucent water.** The water tile draws as a tint over the back layer.
6. **One persistent flag per room** for the fire. Pots and dust reset on re-entry, as
   bulbs do.
7. **Two state machines and a shoal** in life.c; two voice palettes and a dozen prop
   sounds in audio.c; both ambiences remade.
8. The start and the lamp move; route.py's hop D starts from col 2; PREMISE.md rewritten.
