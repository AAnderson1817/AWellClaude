# PREMISE — The Vault and the City Under It

Chosen after the stone was played. This replaces the salt-works premise (Brine and
Ballast), which belonged to the parked two-verb build and is kept whole at
`parked/PREMISE-brine-and-ballast.md`. The restart never re-chose a setting; the user chose
this one, and the two rooms are being redressed to it. If an implementation detail
conflicts with this file, this file wins or the change gets recorded here with a reason.

## Setting (one sentence)
Treasure hunters opened a vault and it sealed behind them; under it is an ancient city of
the strange people who built it, and of the hunters who came before and never left.

## Tone
Alice's whimsy: things behaving with a confident logic that is not yours. First contact's
intrigue: a people you learn only by what they do, because you share no language. Dread
from indifference (L9): the natives are larger than you and mostly unbothered; the
trapped hunters are your size and your shape and are not trying to leave.

## No words, anywhere (L5)
Inhabitants speak the way the plant does: a voice of syllables through a formant, a body
that gestures, and an answer to what you set down. Hold is the conversation: you put the
lamp or a stone in front of something and see what it does with it. Trade without words.

## The light rule (the coupling)
Amber is flame, and flame is the hunters': your lamp, the campfire, the seams in raw rock.
Green-white is the city's own light: its glass lamps, the face, the native's flank, the
pigment of its murals, the fish. Two light bakes, never mixed. You learn who made a thing
by what colour it gives off. Protect this: nothing of theirs glows amber, nothing of yours
glows green.

## Verb A — Hold (built)
One hand. The lamp, which gives light and floats; a stone, which sinks and takes you down
with it. Weight touches the gravity axis only (D4, carried over). Second readings arrive
with the inhabitants: things that answer light (the fire, the mural, the windows, the
native) and things that answer a stone set down (the hunter's cairn, the face).

## Verb B — open
Not designed until the two rooms are dense and played. The candidate is Call.

## The world
25 rooms is the ceiling (L1). Two exist: the Vault Mouth and the Drowned Quarter, designed
in `claude/ROOMS.md` as response tables. Massive is conveyed, not built: windows onto
spaces you cannot enter, lights behind grilles, a face too large for its room.

## Layer 2 hook
Not yet. Whatever it is, it is visible in the first two minutes and usable by the end and
never announced (L8). The door is the first candidate: shut, and the only thing in the
game that never answers.

## Decisions
**D5 — The start is at the door.** You begin on the step beside the shut door with your
lamp at your feet. The reset returns you there.
**D6 — The fire persists.** The campfire your lamp lights stays lit, like an item keeps
its place. The reset puts it out, because the reset is everything as it began.
**D7 — Geometry is frozen through the redesign.** Platforms, the shaft, the water and
the bulbs are played and signed off; `tools/route.py` and `tools/escape.py` guard them.
Dressing that needs a one-tile change is allowed if both stay green.
