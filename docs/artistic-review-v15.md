## Findings

**1. The hunter is integrated; the original tending defect is resolved within the tested scope.** In v14 the camp was uninhabited. V15 initializes and steps the hunter, renders the same detached state in both modes, uses actual cairn/offer items and supplies syllabic audio. In [tend frame 2286](evidence/hunter/native-v15/tend/f2286.png), the fixed arms fold around a stone held at the belly, instead of lengthening into the diagonal rods seen in the rejected first candidate. The actual stone returns to the pile. The hat and pack distinguish the hunter while preserving its relation to the player; there is no new emissive character beacon, text, combat, camera motion or extra verb.

The current module evidence covers immediate taken-voice priority, cancellation of obsolete social requests, pitched mouth timing and real-item conservation. It therefore supersedes the first candidate's delayed sharp response, rather than leaving that finding silently unresolved. Technical timing is not listening approval. The separate [presentation review](hunter-rendering-review.md) gives the exact fixed-arm tests and inspected frames.

The gesture remains simple. The stone briefly masks the eyes during transfer; entry into the grip is not a blended grasp. Sitting is communicated by a rigid lean and tucked feet. Four equally tilted ellipsoids still read as a tall egg stack rather than the authored flat stones, and [warm idle](evidence/hunter/native-v15/fire/f0160.png) loses much of the left body/pack behind it. These are visible craft limits, not failures of item conservation. Future stone refinement must retain one rendered object per real item and its pickup bounds.

**2. The foreground geology remains the clearest environment mismatch.** The large raw-rock areas around the Vault door and floor retain broad flat planes, continuous borders and limited internal depth. They read as broken panels inside an inherited tile silhouette. The raw-rock reference instead shows unequal interlocking masses, overlapping fractured returns, and secondary wear concentrated around a few dominant clefts. The current material grain cannot create that volume. This is a construction problem inside and behind the protected contact outline; it does not justify changing landings or filling every surface with chips. A few convincing rock-to-timber and rock-to-door junctions at the actual camera are more valuable than more global noise, bloom or rubble.

**3. The Drowned face remains below the required hero-asset standard.** Its eyes, orbital metalwork and nose now have real volume, but the broad cheeks, rounded nasal base, lower lip and chin still read as softly modeled clay. The reference carries stronger carved planes, stone returns and a more convincing transition from monument to surrounding masonry. At the full camera those primary differences remain visible even through the water. The existing eye response is readable and appropriately restrained; adding anger, extra glow or uniformly deep cracks would change the intended indifference without fixing the sculpture. Ongoing isolated studies are not delivered content and receive no credit in this verdict.

**4. Construction quality varies enough to weaken cohesion.** Unequal city courses, seated cornices, narrow rear piers and mineral texture are useful progress, but large ashlar fronts and repeated horizontal beds still expose the modular assembly. The distant city has richer architectural layering than several foreground interfaces. Square green fixture plates with pale marks remain schematic beside modeled stone. The mural's pale scalloped backing reads as a separate plaque when its pigment disappears. The thin shaft bars, highly regular shelves and simple pots do not yet share the detailed construction of the governing references. These objects exist as geometry; their presence and triangle counts do not establish production-level finish.

**5. The living-world scope remains materially incomplete.** The hunter must no longer be listed as absent. The aquatic native is still absent from the current simulation and captures: no indifferent circuit, breath, light conversation, stone investigation or fish response to that inhabitant. Those behaviors are central to the flooded room's scale and mystery. The Drowned `PROPS` grid remains empty; the authored falling roof pots, throat rope, banners and lost-pack response are unfinished. Face-mouth bubbles and the native's breath are also not delivered. Conversely, windows, facade openings, cornices, column, water, fronds, eight fish and the face response are present and must not be incorrectly counted as missing. Completing one inhabitant does not complete either room's full response table or the final sound pass.

**6. Depth, restraint and thematic seeds work, but immersion remains a hypothesis.** Open background between narrow supports, warm hunter light against cool city sources, and the readable waterline establish distance and quiet focus. The lamp's concealment of the procession, windows' withdrawal, and the face's answer to a sinking stone provide unannounced second readings of Hold. The cairn now adds care and habit to the faith theme. Much of the astronomical metalwork remains an ornamental cue, however; whether these cues form a compelling mystery rather than unrelated decoration requires observation. More brightness, constant reactions or more visible rewards would not establish that connection.

## Design-law validation and next acceptance evidence

The [final headless summary](evidence/hunter/final-headless/summary.json) reports sanitizer checks, 35 routes, 36 escape surfaces and six actual CLI hunter cases. Historical comparisons cover 21,160 headless frames; the [rendered report](evidence/hunter/render-preservation.json) covers 6,640 frames with the final native executable. The [module report](evidence/hunter/integrated-module-tests.json) includes 28,748 conservation steps and 3,276 actual carried-stone arm solves, including the full six-stone case, with no unreachable contacts. These are substantial technical safeguards, not evidence that a stranger enjoyed, understood or became absorbed in the world.

The four new real cairn stones are an intentional world extension. Near the cairn, original Hold can now select one of them where the old world had decoration; the complete-item differential records that difference. “Every gameplay trace is unchanged” would be an inaccurate claim. No new gate or redundant verb is introduced. The second verb remains deliberately open in [PREMISE](../claude/PREMISE.md); two rooms exist under a 25-room ceiling. A finished first layer, credits and an invisible deeper hook remain unfinished. The historical surprise log is not a substitute for observing discoveries in this revised world.

The five human tests in [DESIGN-LAW](../claude/DESIGN-LAW.md) remain open: unexpected toy use, a convincing finish followed by an unannounced deeper reading, wordless control learning, unease without a named threat, and unplanned traversal. This review did not conduct blind play, perceptual listening, a complete browser playthrough or a sustained motion-comfort session. Browser smoke evidence belongs to its separate verification record.

The next acceptance evidence should combine improved representative foreground/hero construction with the completed authored inhabitants and responses, then observe unbriefed players. Record their mistaken hypotheses, the world's acknowledgments, discoveries, route reading, listening comfort and unanswered questions without supplying the intended interpretation. V15 is a useful integration checkpoint; **the overall request remains in rework**.
