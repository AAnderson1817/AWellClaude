The works

Five rooms by five. Dry terraces and collapsing scaffold at the top, evaporation pans in the middle, and at the bottom the deep brine, where a full body sinks and walks the pan floor. You start in the middle. There are no locked doors anywhere in it — every room can be reached from every other at any weight.

Twenty-five rooms, built fully open. Not one door is locked and not one route needs an item you do not already have — what your weight changes is which way is easy, never which way is possible. Can you get lost in it, and does it hold together as one place?

Four things worth trying, none of which any room tells you about. Hang from a corner with full kettles and wait. Get into deep brine empty, bite something below the waterline, pull down, and let go. Stand heavy on a pale crust span until it drops you, then look at the shape of the hole. And walk a crust span heavy without ever stopping — it is not your weight the salt is counting.
