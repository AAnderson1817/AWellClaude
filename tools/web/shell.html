The room

A sandbox, not a design. A pale crust bridge over a pit on the left and a crust shelf above it; a shallow pan with an iron lip at each end; a terrace and a deep pan on the right. Nothing here is a puzzle — it is somewhere to find out what weight does.

Weight is the verb. Run speed, acceleration and friction are identical at every load on purpose — only the gravity axis moves, so the kettles never start doing the second verb's job. Does the dial feel worth turning?

Two things worth trying, neither of which any room tells you about: stand still on the pale bridge with full kettles, then try crossing the same bridge without stopping. And look at how deep you sit in a pan — that is the only load counter there is.
