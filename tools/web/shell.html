The room

Floor gaps of two, three and four tiles; a one, two and three tile step; two thin ledges; a black shelf; water. It is a calibration rig, not a design.

Movement is the foundation and the hardest thing to change later, so this is the one thing I want your verdict on before anything else gets built on top of it. Does it feel right?

Useful answers are things like: floaty at the apex, too slippery stopping, jump too short for the room, turnaround too sharp. Any number in that table can move.
